KHARVIE — OFFICIAL BIOGRAPHY

Kharvie is a singer and songwriter from Delta State. His real name is Victor Avannah. Born on April 12, 2003, Kharvie is part of a new generation of artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats.
