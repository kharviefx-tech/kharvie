Artist profile

Kharvie, whose real name is Victor Avannah, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, he is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats.

A new-generation Afrobeats voice.
