Short bio

Extended bio

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is a Nigerian singer and songwriter from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, Kharvie is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats. His artistic direction centers on melody, rhythm and contemporary Nigerian energy, with a growing focus on music, visual identity, live performance and meaningful audience connection. Kharvie is building an independent artist platform designed to support releases, collaborations, media opportunities and long-term creative growth.
