Kharvie, whose real name is Victor Avannah, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria.

About the Artist

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats. Born on April 12, 2003, he is building his identity through original music, songwriting and contemporary African sounds.
