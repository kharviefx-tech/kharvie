Victor Avannah

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria.

About Kharvie

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats. Born on April 12, 2003, he is developing his artistic identity through original music, songwriting and contemporary African sounds.

Kharvie's work is rooted in melody, emotion and relatable storytelling, with an approach that reflects the energy of modern Nigerian popular music while continuing to develop his own sound and identity.
