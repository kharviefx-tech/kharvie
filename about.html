Overview

Kharvie is the professional name of Victor Avannah, a singer-songwriter from Delta State, Nigeria. His work is associated with Afrobeats and contemporary R&B.

His musical direction combines rhythmic elements associated with Afrobeats with melodic and songwriting influences from R&B.
