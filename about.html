Who is Kharvie?

Kharvie, whose real name is Victor Avannah, is a singer and songwriter from Delta State. Born on April 12, 2003, Victor Avannah is part of a new generation of artists developing contemporary African music and shaping his own artistic identity.

Kharvie's musical direction draws from Afrobeats and Afrobeat-inspired sounds, combining melody, emotion, rhythm and relatable storytelling.

As an independent artist, Kharvie is developing his catalogue and official digital presence across major music and social platforms.
