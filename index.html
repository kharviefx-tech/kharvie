02 · About Kharvie

Meet the artist.

Kharvie, whose real name is Victor Avannah, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, he is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeat.

The official website brings together his music, visual work, professional information, press resources and booking opportunities in one place.
