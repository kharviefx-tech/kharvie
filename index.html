ABOUT
KHARVIE

Kharvie is a Nigerian singer and songwriter from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, he represents a new generation of Nigerian artists contributing to the evolving sound of contemporary African music.

His music combines infectious rhythms, melodic songwriting and modern production, with an artistic direction rooted in African influences and contemporary Afrobeat.
