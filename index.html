ABOUT KHARVIE

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie.

Kharvie is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, he is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats.

His music is built around melody, rhythm and relatable storytelling, with a sound that continues to grow through each release. This website is the official home for his music, videos, appearances and artist information.
