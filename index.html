ABOUT KHARVIE

VICTOR AVANNAH.
KNOWN AS KHARVIE.

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria.

Born on April 12, 2003, Kharvie is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats, with a focus on melody, rhythm and relatable African storytelling.
