THE ARTIST

About Kharvie

Kharvie is a Nigerian singer and songwriter creating music rooted in rhythm, identity and authentic expression.

Active since 2020, Kharvie blends Afrobeat, Street Pop, Trap, Hip-Hop and R&B influences to create a sound that reflects his journey and artistic identity.

This official website is the home of Kharvie's music, videos, news, releases and official updates.
