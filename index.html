07 · THE ARTIST

Victor Avannah,
known as Kharvie.

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria. Born on April 12, 2003, he is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats.

Kharvie's work is centred on melody, rhythm and relatable storytelling, with a sound that continues to develop across contemporary African music.
