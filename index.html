THE ARTIST

About Kharvie

Kharvie is the artist name of Victor Avannah, a Nigerian singer and songwriter from Delta State, Nigeria.

Born on April 12, 2003, Kharvie creates contemporary African music with a sound shaped by Afrobeat, melodic songwriting, emotion and relatable storytelling.

His music explores themes of love, ambition, self-discovery and everyday experiences. Kharvie continues to develop his identity as an independent Nigerian artist.
