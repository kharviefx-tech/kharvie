06 / THE ARTIST

WHO IS
KHARVIE?

Victor Avannah, professionally known as Kharvie, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter and recording artist from Delta State, Nigeria.

Born on April 12, 2003, Kharvie is part of a new generation of Nigerian artists shaping the evolving sound of Afrobeats. His work is built around melody, contemporary African sounds and relatable storytelling as he develops his artistic identity.

This official profile connects the professional name Kharvie with the real name Victor Avannah and serves as a central reference for his music, biography and official platforms.
